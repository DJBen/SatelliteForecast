In the app

Four screens, one evening.

01 · Forecast

Your next observation, already waiting.

Open the app and the next visible pass is on screen: time, duration, peak height and a preview of where it rises and sets. The rest of the week sits below, grouped by day.

02 · Sky chart

Follow the path across the stars.

Stars, constellations, the Milky Way and the planets, with the station's track drawn through them. Compass mode points the phone at it.

03 · Planetarium

Stand under it before you go outside.

A 3D sky you can turn with a finger, showing where the station will be against the horizon you actually have.

04 · Widgets

Tonight's answer on the Home Screen.

Small, medium and large widgets show the next pass with its arc and times. No need to open the app to know whether to step outside.
