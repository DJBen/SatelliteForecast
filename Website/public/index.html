In the app

Four screens, one evening.

01 · Forecast

Your next observation, already waiting.

The next visible pass is on screen the moment you open the app: time, duration, peak height and where it rises and sets.

02 · Sky chart

Follow the path across the stars.

Constellations, the Milky Way and the planets, with the station's track drawn through them. Compass mode points the phone at it.

03 · Planetarium

Stand under it before you go outside.

A 3D sky you can turn with a finger, showing where the station will be against your real horizon.

04 · Widgets

Tonight's answer on the Home Screen.

Small, medium and large widgets show the next pass with its arc and times, so you know whether to step outside.
