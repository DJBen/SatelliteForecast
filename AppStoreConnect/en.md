Promotional Text

Description
Space Station Passes predicts ISS and Tiangong passes over you with useful infographics and much more.
- Pass prediction: predict satellite passes for ISS, Tiangong and 4000+ satellites over any geographical location globally.
- Sky chart: pinpoint the trail of satellite pass with detailed interactive star chart.
- Notifications: timely notifications for ISS and Tiangong passes; set alarm for individual satellite passes, so you won't miss the moment.
- Night mode: preserve your night vision while using the app.
- Realtime sky: This is an experimental feature that shows all the currently passing satellites above you.
