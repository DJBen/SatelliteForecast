# Adaptive Sun label review

Captured on iPhone 17 Pro Max in dark mode. The interactive review fixture renders
JD 2459373.3333333 at latitude 37.49, longitude -122.23; its clock UI retains the
fixture pass time and does not describe the overridden rendered date.

- `daytime-wide-dark.jpg`: 35° field of view, dark text against solar glare.
- `daytime-zoom-dark.jpg`: 2° field of view, pale text against blue sky.

The 15 pt text has no stroke or shadow. Spacing clears the projected solar radius
(minimum 17 pt) by 10 pt. Color is selected from estimated local atmosphere and
solar aureole luminance.
