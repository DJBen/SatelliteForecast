# Tiangong icon studies

Built-in imagegen exploration using candidate B as material, color and atmosphere reference. Production icon unchanged.

Simplified three-module T configuration, based on https://www.cmse.gov.cn/fxrw/mengtian/mtjj/202211/t20221106_51285.html . These are stylized concepts, not engineering renderings. T2 and T4 introduce additional panel segmentation; refine geometry after selecting a composition.

## Prompts

### T1 — Face-on

Use case: precise-object-edit. Square app icon exploration. Reference image is approved candidate B, use as palette, material and background reference. Replace its generic diagonal satellite with the fully assembled Tiangong space station, simplified into an iconic low-detail glass sculpture. Preserve B's midnight indigo background and shallow curved Earth limb across lower quarter glowing warm ivory-peach, coral, magenta, violet. Station material same translucent pastel ivory/pink/lilac glass, broad clean facets and soft gradients. Anatomical structure: three connected pressurized modules form a T, Tianhe central stem joined at its forward node to opposed coaxial Wentian and Mengtian lab modules forming crossbar; four long broad rectangular solar wings in two paired sets attached at the OUTER ends of the lab modules. Clearly connected station, not separate floating polygons or generic satellite. Simplify modules into chunky lightly faceted tubes with tapered ends; panels into smooth glass ribbons. Use fully assembled three-module station without visiting vehicles. Keep bold simple silhouette, no tiny details, no panel grids, no text, no stars, no flags, no antenna clutter, no hatches, no photo textures, no complex specular caustics, no decorative fragments. Strong small-size readability, whole station visible with 12% edge margin. Full bleed square no rounded mask. View nearly orthographic from above the station plane, symmetric face-on T structure, lab crossbar horizontal, central core extends downward toward viewer. Array pairs on outer lab ends extend up and down in image, all six major masses legible. Refined flat glass graphic.
