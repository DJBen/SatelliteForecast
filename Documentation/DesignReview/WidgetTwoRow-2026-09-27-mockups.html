Station Widget Plan

The Space Station Home Screen widgets: what shipped for the two-row small and medium layouts, and the review of the remaining two configurations, the small pass chart and the large sky chart, from device captures on September 27.

Shipped · small two-row and medium

Small. The sooner pass leads: icon, name, elevation chip, large rounded time, then "Tonight · 11 min". The other station sits in the footer with its time and day.
Medium. One row per station: icon, name with route and duration, right-aligned time with its day, and the elevation on the arc. System content margins are replaced by 10 pt top and bottom.

S1Same hierarchy as the small two-row: name and chip on top, big time with "Tonight · 11 min" under it, and the dome as the bottom third with only compass letters. The elevation moves from the arc to the chip, so the arc no longer needs headroom for a label and can sit lower and wider.
  • The arc floats. A 27° pass is a low arc, and centring the "peak label plus arc plus compass" band leaves dead space above and below. Anchoring the dome to the bottom edge and moving the degrees into the chip fixes both.
  • No day, no duration. "7:17 PM" alone does not say tonight or how long; the two-row small already carries "Tonight · 11 min", so the chart should too.
  • The time is small and teal. It is the most useful number and should be the largest thing, as it is in the two-row layout.
  • Live state. At 7:20 the pass had already risen but the header still read 7:17 PM. Timeline entries exist at rise and set, so this is either WidgetKit lagging on the reload or the entry not marking the pass as in progress. Recommendation: while a pass is up, replace the time with a filled LIVE pill and a countdown rendered with a timer-style text so it updates without a reload, and add a marker on the arc at the current position.

L1Header gains the station icon, a subtitle with day, route, duration and peak elevation, and a live pill with the time left when the pass is up. On the chart: labels are placed inward along the radial direction, the shadow-entry point keeps its marker but drops its label when it sits within two minutes of the set, and a bright dot marks the station's current position.
  • Overlapping labels at the rim. "7:26 PM" and "7:28 PM" stack on top of each other and cover the E marker. The anti-collision pass only slides labels vertically; at the rim it should push inward along the radius, and events closer than two minutes should share one label or show a marker only.
  • Header is thin for the space. Name and time only. The large widget can afford the same subtitle the app's row uses, plus the peak elevation, so the reader does not have to find it on the arc.
  • No sense of "now". During the pass the chart looks identical to an hour before. A current-position dot and a live pill make the widget worth glancing at while it matters.
  • Moon at the limb. The Moon renders half outside the disk at the eastern horizon. Clip bodies to the disk with a soft edge instead of drawing them over the rim.

Recommendation

Do S1 first: it is the small two-row layout with the dome in place of the footer, so it shares code and needs no new data. Then L1: the header change is quick; the radial label placement is the real work and also improves the store screenshot. The live state (pill, countdown, current-position dot) applies to all four configurations and is worth doing once for all of them, with timeline entries kept at rise and set and a timer-style text for the countdown.
