The two-row Space Station widgets in the small and medium sizes: the problems with the previous layout, the two layouts chosen, and the native renders as shipped. Data is the live Redwood City forecast from the 2.0.0 captures: ISS tonight 8:04 PM, 48°, NW → SE, 7 minutes; Tiangong Sep 30, 5:41 AM, 20°, SW → E.

  • No hierarchy. The next pass and a pass four days out look the same. The widget should answer "is there something tonight?" at a glance.
  • The time is not the hero. It is set in the same size as the name, and mixed formats ("8:04 PM" vs "9/30, 5:41 AM") sit at different widths.
  • The teal dot carries no information. It reads as a bullet. The glass station icons from the app would identify the station faster than the name.
  • Empty space in the wrong places. Small has a large gap around the separator; medium has a gap between the name and the time.
  • Quality is hidden. Peak elevation, the thing that tells you whether to go outside, is a tiny label on medium and absent on small.

As shipped

Native renders from the widget preview test on the iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator, dark mode, at 170 × 170 and 364 × 170 points. Both use the fixture pass, so the two medium rows show the same time.

Small. ISS leads with 7:46 PM and "Tonight · SW → NE · 11 min"; Tiangong footer with its time and day stacked right.
Medium. Icon, name and route on the left; right-aligned time with its day; elevation on the arc.

Fit was checked in en, es, fr, pt-BR, ru, ja, ko and zh-Hans at normal, XXXL and accessibility sizes, including the in-progress, empty and setup states; all 48 renders stay inside the widget bounds. In-progress passes show "In progress" once, in the time slot, with the day dropped from the subtitle.

Rules carried into the implementation

  • Elevation chip rule: filled accent at 40° and above, outlined below, matching the app's list.
  • Time rule: today shows the time only; other days show the day above or beside a time in the same size, never a slash date in the time slot.
  • Icon rule: teal ISS, peach Tiangong, the same renders as the app so colour alone distinguishes the stations before the name is read.
  • Arc rule: labels come off the tiny medium arcs; the chip and subtitle already carry those numbers. The dotted horizon and shadow segments stay.
