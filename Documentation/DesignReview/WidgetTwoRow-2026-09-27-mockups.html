Station Widget Plan

The Space Station Home Screen widgets: what shipped for the two-row small and medium layouts, and the review of the remaining two configurations, the small pass chart and the large sky chart, from device captures on September 27.

Shipped · small two-row and medium

Small. The sooner pass leads: icon, name, elevation chip, large rounded time, then "Tonight · 11 min". The other station sits in the footer with its time and day.
Medium. One row per station: icon, name with route and duration, right-aligned time with its day, and the elevation on the arc. System content margins are replaced by 10 pt top and bottom.
Elevation chipfilled accent at 40° and above, outlined below Timetoday shows the time only; other days show the day beneath or beside it; in-progress passes say so once, in the time slot Iconsteal ISS, peach Tiangong, the same renders as the app Fit48 renders across eight locales, three text sizes and the in-progress, empty and setup states stay inside bounds; 292 pt medium widgets drop the arc, tight small footers drop the day

Shipped · small pass chart and large sky chart

Small chart (S1). The two-row small's hierarchy with the dome in place of the footer: icon, name and elevation chip, large time, "Tonight · 11 min", and the arc anchored to the bottom with compass letters only. The degrees live in the chip, so the arc needs no headroom and sits lower and wider.
Large (L1, without live state). Header gains the station icon, the compact name in the same rounded bold as the other sizes, "Tonight · 11 min" beneath it, and the time in matching weight. On the chart, colliding labels are pushed inward along their radius, and a shadow crossing within two minutes of the rise or set keeps its marker but drops its label.

Verified in the eight-locale matrix at normal, XXXL and accessibility sizes, including the in-progress, empty and setup states: 48 renders inside bounds. At the 146 pt small width a long station name next to the chip still truncates to "Tiang…"; the 170 pt width used by current iPhones is unaffected.

Deferred

  • Live state. A LIVE pill with time remaining and a current-position dot during the pass. Held until we know how reliably WidgetKit delivers the rise-time entry; a timer-style countdown text would update without a reload.
  • Moon at the limb. Bodies still draw over the disk rim at the horizon; clip with a soft edge.
