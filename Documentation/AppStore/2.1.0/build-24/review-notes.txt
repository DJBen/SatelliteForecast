Version 2.1.0 build 24. No account or login is required. Choose an observer location in Settings or allow location access.

The home screen displays forecast weather beside the selected location. Tap the weather row for details and source attribution. Weather is loaded from our Firebase backend using approximate coordinates and a separate anonymous session. Automatic ISS/Tiangong reminders include brief weather guidance and are skipped when fresh forecasts show a high probability of rain throughout the visible pass. Manually scheduled individual-pass reminders remain unchanged. Updated home screenshots use illustrative weather with the previously reviewed orbital/observer moments.

Open Sky now for the live planetarium. Passing now opens the searchable satellite list; the adjacent filter controls distance and unlit satellites. Tap a satellite to see its trajectory and use the target button to center it. Tapping the selected satellite details opens the optional brightness-report slider; Report sends the observation to our Firebase backend with installation and messaging identifiers, as described in the privacy policy.
