Sky Now is now a permanent second tab with a live 3D planetarium showing satellites currently above your horizon. Pan, zoom, follow your device, and select a passing satellite. Sky Now always follows the current time.

For this build, Sky Now loads at most 100 bright low-Earth-orbit satellites. Switching tabs reuses the loaded catalog, and returning to Passes reuses fresh forecasts instead of recalculating them. Leaving a tab cancels unfinished work.

Catalog metadata now uses an isolated SQLite actor and an in-memory cache. Orbital timestamp parsing is faster, and satellite inclination metadata has been corrected.

Please test first load, switching tabs while loading, returning to Sky Now and Passes, location changes, background/foreground transitions, and offline use after a successful load.
